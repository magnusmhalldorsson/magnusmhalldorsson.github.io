SWAT 2016

15th Scandinavian Symposium and Workshops on Algorithm Theory
June 22-24, 2016, Reykjavik, Iceland

Registration

Registration can be done either by a credit card or bank transfer, through a secure website.

Please visit the registration website, after reading the following information.

The early registration deadline is 22.05.2016. The fees (in Icelandic Kronas) are specified below.

Rate Early (until 22.05) Late (23.05 to 20.06)
Regular ISK 46.000 ISK 55.000
Student ISK 31.000 ISK 40.000
Guest ISK 14.000 ISK 14.000

On-site registration is possible from June 21th, with a higher fee.

Conference fee includes conference, lunch and coffeebreaks during the conference, conference dinner and excursion.
The fee for a guest/accompanying person includes conference dinner and excursion.

Cancellation policy: Written cancellations received before June 10th will be refunded in full (after deducting bank transfer fees) and the ones received from June 11th to June 19th will be refunded in half. After June 19th, no refunds will be granted.

SWAT'16 registrations are handled by Islandsmot/Atlantik, a local licensed conference organizer.
