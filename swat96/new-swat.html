Fifth Scandinavian Workshop on Algorithm Theory

SWAT is intended as a forum for researchers in the area of design and analysis of algorithms and data structures. It covers all areas in this field, including combinatorics, computational geometry, data bases, and parallel and distributed computing.

SWAT '96 will be held in Reykjavik, Iceland during July 3-5, 1996.

Submission deadline: February 20

Call for Papers

Location

Iceland is a dream location for nature lovers, with its unspoiled nature, dramatic landscape, and unfettered scenery. Reykjavik is the northernmost capital in the world, but as the hub of the country and of Icelandic culture worldwide, it boasts cultural events and city life comparable with considerably larger cities. The white nights during the conference period should allow for plenty of opportunities for both scenery and people watching.

Getting there

Icelandair offers frequent connections from both sides of the N-Atlantic. In fact, for a competitive cross-Atlantic fare, you may stop-over in Reykjavik during the conference. We have obtained special conference prices for participants. Please check the nearest Icelandair sales office and mention the SWAT name.

Surface travel is also possible. A ferry connects Eastern Iceland to Norway, Denmark, and Scotland via the Faroe Islands. Also, Eimskip offers passenger rooms on their freighters from N-America and Europe.

Climate

Thanks to the Gulf stream, Iceland is not as cold as its northernly position might suggest. The average July temperature is comfortable, dry 11C. The weather is, however, unpredictable and visitors should be prepared for the unexpected. A water-resistant coat or jacket is useful, but not an umbrella. Bring a swimsuit for the geothermally heated pools.
