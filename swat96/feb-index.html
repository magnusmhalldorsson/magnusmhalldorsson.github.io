Fifth Scandinavian Workshop on Algorithm Theory
SWAT '96

Preliminary Information

SWAT is intended as a forum for researchers in the area of design and analysis of algorithms and data structures. It covers all areas in this field, including combinatorics, computational geometry, data bases, and parallel and distributed computing. SWAT '96 will be held in Reykjavik, Iceland during July 3-5, 1996.
