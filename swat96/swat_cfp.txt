		    CALL FOR PAPERS	

	Fifth Scandinavian Workshop on Algorithm Theory

		       (SWAT'96)

		    July 3-5, 1996

	          Reykjavik, Iceland

The workshop, which alternates with the Workshop on Algorithms and Data
Structures (WADS), is intended as a forum for researchers in the area of
design and analysis of algorithms and data structures. We invite submissions
of papers presenting original research on algorithms and data structures in
all areas, including computational geometry, parallel and distributed
computing, graph theory, and combinatorics.  Contributors should send five
hard copies of a full paper (not exceeding 12 pages) to

	Rolf Karlsson
	Department of Computer Science
	Lund University
	Box 118 (Ole Romers v 3)
	S-221 00 Lund, SWEDEN

	Email: rolf@dna.lth.se, fax: +46-46-131021.

Submissions must arrive by February 20, 1996.
The submissions should give the author's email address and fax number if
available. Authors will be notified of acceptance or rejection by April 1, 1996.
Proceedings will be published, presumably in the Springer-Verlag series
Lecture Notes in Computer Science. The final versions of accepted papers must
arrive in camera-ready form before April 25, 1996.
