Travel and Accomodation

Conference site

SWAT '96 will be held from July 3 to 5 at the University of Iceland in Reykjavik. The campus is about 7 minutes by foot south of city center. The workshop will be held at the Oddi building.

Surface travel

Surface travel is also possible. A ferry connects Eastern Iceland to Norway, Denmark, and Scotland via the Faroe Islands. Also, Eimskip offers passenger rooms on their freighters from N-America and Europe.

Airport travel

The airport shuttle leaves about half our after landing of each flight, taking you to Hotel Loftleidir in Reykjavik. The trip takes 30 minutes and the cost is $7 one way.

Accomodation

We have reservations at the following two hotels, from July 2 to July 6.
Hotel Gardur
$65 single, $82 double.
Hotel Loftleidir
$115 single, $150 double.
Both of these include breakfast.

Hotel Gardur is a dormitory on campus converted to a summer hotel. It is centrally located both for the conference and for the city center. It offers simple yet comfortable rooms furnished with wash basin. Bath and shower facilities can be found on each floor, and a TV lounge stands at guests' disposal.

Hotel Loftleidir is a full-service conference hotel, with private bath or shower, minibar, phone, and other modern conveniences. The rooms have TVs with both local and foreign channels. The hotel has four restaurants, inside swimming pool, jacuzzi, sauna, massage, solarium, hairdresser service, an exercise room, and a souvenir shop and newsstand. City center can be reached by foot or by bus. The hotel stands both by south seaside and by Oskjuhlid Hill park, with walking paths leading to panoramic views.

For those seeking cheaper lodging, we suggest the Salvation Army, right in the center of town, with friendly staff and clean rooms at $45. On the other end of the scale is Hotel Saga, on the edge of campus, with single rooms at around $200.

Climate

Thanks to the Gulf stream, Iceland is not as cold as its northernly position might suggest. The average July temperature is comfortable, bdry 11C. The weather is, however, unpredictable and visitors should be prepared for the unexpected. A water-resistant coat or jacket is useful, but not an umbrella. Bring a swimsuit for the geothermally heated pools.
