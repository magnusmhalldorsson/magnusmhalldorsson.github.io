Things to do in Reykjavik

Iceland is place like nowhere else. As the Harvard Guide to Europe said it once, if you've ever experienced anything like the landscape, the climate, or the prices in Iceland, you've been there. A few meager years have seen commodity prices stumble to less than average N-European levels, but the nature doesn't change as easily. A homecoming unfailingly causes a tug at the soft spots in a returning native, be it the gusty freshness or the crude beauty of the nature.

Reykjavik is not just the center of the country in all senses of the word, but also the center of the Icelandic language and culture. As such, it offers noticably more than most cities of comparable size.

Places to go and things to do during off hours/days:

  • The Pearl: An observation deck with 360 degrees view over the city, the mountains, glacier, and nearby counties.
  • Swimming: Geothermal heat makes outside pools possible all-year-round.
  • Horse Riding
  • Cycling The city has recently made major improvements in trails for cycling and walking. There are new trails lining the whole southward seaside, reaching out east all the way into the municipal natural park of Heidmork.
  • Nightlife: Not for the faint of heart.
  • Go climb a mountain! Hitch a ride out of the city, and just head up!
  • Blue Lagoon: Hot tubbing in the middle of nowhere. Out of this world.
  • Strolling Downtown: Quaintness takes on a new meaning.
  • Galleries and Musems
  • Golfing at midnight: Literally.
  • Go wild! Rafting, Fly fishing, Snowmobiling on glaciers...
