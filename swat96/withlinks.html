Fifth Scandinavian Workshop on Algorithm Theory (SWAT '96)

Preliminary Information

SWAT is intended as a forum for researchers in the area of design and analysis of algorithms and data structures. It covers all areas in this field, including combinatorics, computational geometry, data bases, and parallel and distributed computing. SWAT '96 will be held in Reykjavik, Iceland during July 3-5, 1996 (tentative).

Iceland is a dream location for nature lovers, with its unspoiled nature, dramatic landscape, and unfettered scenery. Reykjavik is the northernmost capital in the world, but as the hub of the country and of Icelandic culture worldwide, it boasts cultural events and city life comparable with considerably larger cities. The white nights during the conference period should allow for plenty of opportunities for both scenery and people watching.
